Three Headline Operational Findings

  1. Ignoring open cases understates processing time by 41%. Naive closed-case medians report 46.0 days, whereas Kaplan–Meier survival analysis right-censoring the 27,542 still-open petitions (53.0%) yields a true median of 65.0 days (+19 days).
  2. Cross-county disparities reflect both operational pace and local clerk logging practices. Closed petitions resolve fastest in rural jurisdictions (Allegany 12d, Cecil 15d) and slowest in high-volume urban dockets (Anne Arundel 55d, Baltimore City 54d, Prince George's 53d). Crucially, Baltimore City records almost no Cancelled (61) or Expired (0) warrant returns in this feed—making raw clearance-rate comparisons misleading without schema audits.
  3. Hybrid rule + LLM extraction beats LLM-only deployment. On 60 held-out Maryland appellate orders, deterministic regex matched or beat the LLM on structured docket IDs (0.950 F1, 0.15 ms, $0) and standard decretals (1.000 F1), while gemini-3.8-flash delivered a +24.5 Macro-F1 lift on nuanced remand detection (0.983 vs. 0.738) with 0.0% unsupported citations.

What This Data Can and Cannot Support

  • Can support: Quantifying survivorship bias in court time standards, diagnosing cross-county operational bottlenecks and clerk logging inconsistencies, and selecting field-level rule vs. LLM extraction policies before deployment.
  • Cannot support: Causal claims about judicial bias, landlord intent, or tenant outcomes. Public records omit tenant counsel status, rent arrears, informal payment agreements, and Baltimore City warrant cancellations.

Privacy Controls Enforced in SQL

  • Downstream scripts query only v_analyst_cases (salted SHA-256 case_id_hash, raw city & 5-digit ZIP removed, coarsened to 3-digit tenant_zip3).
  • Public county/monthly exports enforce strict small-cell suppression (< 10 cases)—suppressing 38 county-category cells and Garrett County's closed-case median (n_closed = 7).
  • Automated assertions in build_warehouse.py fail the build if any PII column or unsuppressed cell leaks.
